In Southampton County, Virginia, a man named Nat Turner waited for a sign.

In Missouri, a farmer named Robert Newsom bought a girl named Celia.

John C. Calhoun called slavery a positive good.

The Cherokee, Creek, Choctaw, Chickasaw and Seminole nations were forced off their homelands.

Traders marched people south in long lines called coffles.

Below the planters were the yeomen. Historians call this paternalism.

On January 1st, 1808, Congress banned the trade. By 1830, the census counted more than 2 million enslaved people. In the 1930s, Harriett Hill was interviewed.
