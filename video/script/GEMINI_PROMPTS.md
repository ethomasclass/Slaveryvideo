# Gemini paintings: Grip Tighter

These are for moments no archive can show. Everything else comes from period images (see `IMAGES.md`).

**How to make them**
- Gemini image generation at its largest size, aspect **16:9**.
- Paste the **whole** prompt, including the style paragraph at the end. It is identical for every image so they read as one series.
- Regenerate rather than fix anything with text, extra fingers, modern objects or wrong details.
- Upload the PNGs with the file names below. They go in `video/public/img/gen/`.
- Every scene still works if you skip an image. **Must-have** marks the ones the video is noticeably worse without.
- **This video's rule:** no suffering bodies and no faces of enslaved people in pain. Show places, objects, hands, and figures from behind or at a distance. On screen, every painting is labeled `Illustration · <what it shows>`.

## 1. `ch01_eclipse.png` (must-have)

**Covers:** ch09, "in February 1831, when the moon slid in front of the sun in the middle of the day and the sky went strange and dim, he took it as the signal." It returns at the end of ch10 ("looked up at a darkened sky").

**Prompt:** February 1831, midday, Southampton County, Virginia. A wide view across flat, winter-bare farm fields edged with dark pine woods. A small cluster of rough log cabins and a split-rail fence sit in the middle distance. The sky is unnaturally dim for noon. High to one side, the sun is almost completely covered by the moon, leaving only a thin, blazing crescent. The landscape is bathed in a strange, cold, gray-gold half-light, with long soft shadows. Three or four small figures stand far off near the cabins, seen from behind, looking up at the sky. Their faces are not visible. The mood is quiet and uneasy.

## 2. `ch03_seeds_by_hand.png`

**Covers:** "It's packed with sticky green seeds. Picking them out by hand, one worker might clean about a pound of cotton a day."

**Prompt:** Georgia, about 1790. A close view of a pair of work-worn hands, a dark-skinned adult's, pulling the small green seeds out of a tuft of raw white cotton fiber over a shallow wooden tray. A small pile of seeds and a small heap of cleaned cotton sit beside the tray on a plain wooden table. Evening light comes from a single tallow candle to one side. Only the hands, forearms and the work are in frame. The mood is patient, tedious, still.

## 3. `ch04_coffle_road.png` (must-have)

**Covers:** "Then they marched them hundreds of miles, chained together in long lines called coffles."

**Prompt:** 1830s, a red-clay road through rolling Southern countryside in late afternoon. A long line of people walks away from the viewer along the road, far in the distance, small against the land: men linked together by a long chain, women and children walking behind, carrying bundles. Two white men on horseback ride beside the line, also seen from behind. A covered wagon follows. Dust hangs in low golden light. No faces are visible; the figures are small and dark against the light. The mood is solemn, heavy, quiet.

## 4. `ch06_weighing_cotton.png` (must-have)

**Covers:** "At the end of every day, each person's cotton was weighed."

**Prompt:** Mississippi, about 1850, dusk at the edge of a cotton field. A tall wooden steelyard scale hangs from a crossbeam beside a weathered gin house. One large woven basket of picked cotton hangs from the scale's hook. A row of full baskets waits on the ground in a line. A white overseer stands beside the scale with an open ledger book and a pencil, seen from behind. In the background, field workers stand at a distance in the fading light, small, their faces not visible. The light is low and orange. The mood is tense and quiet.

## 5. `ch06_cabin_interior.png`

**Covers:** "Families slept in small wooden cabins, often with dirt floors, sometimes two families to a room, on beds of straw and rags."

**Prompt:** A Southern plantation, about 1850. The inside of a small, empty one-room log cabin with a packed-dirt floor. Two pallets of straw and rough rag blankets lie on the floor along the walls. A small brick fireplace holds a few coals. There's a single iron pot, a wooden bucket, a gourd dipper, and a few pieces of clothing on a peg. Pale morning light comes through gaps between the logs and through one small open doorway. No people. The mood is bare and quiet.

## 6. `ch07_empty_cabin_night.png` (must-have)

**Covers:** Celia's story ("So the next time he came to her cabin…"). We have no image of Celia, so this stands in for her, with no people shown.

**Prompt:** Callaway County, Missouri, 1855, night. The outside of a small, solitary brick-and-log cabin at the edge of a farmyard, its single window lit faintly orange from a fireplace inside. A narrow path leads to the door. Bare trees, a split-rail fence, and a large farmhouse far in the background with one dim window. No people. Cold, dark blue night with the faint warm window glow. The mood is lonely and foreboding.

## 7. `ch08_hush_harbor.png` (must-have)

**Covers:** "Some met in secret places in the woods called hush harbors, and turned a big iron pot upside down to muffle the sound of their prayers."

**Prompt:** South Carolina, about 1840, late at night. A small clearing deep in the woods, surrounded by tall trees and hanging moss. A small group of enslaved men and women kneel and sit close together in prayer, seen from behind and in deep shadow, with only their outlines lit. In the foreground, a large black iron cooking pot sits turned upside down on the ground, propped slightly on a stick. A single low lantern glows. Faces are not visible. The mood is hushed, intimate and reverent.

## 8. `ch08_crawl_space.png` (must-have)

**Covers:** "She hid in a crawl space in her grandmother's attic, three feet high at its tallest, for almost seven years. Through a tiny hole, she watched her children play outside."

**Prompt:** Edenton, North Carolina, about 1838. The inside of a tiny, cramped attic crawl space under a sloping roof of rough wooden shingles and rafters, so low that a person could only lie or sit. A thin folded quilt and a small bundle of sewing lie on the bare boards. A single tiny hole in the wall lets in one narrow beam of daylight that falls across the boards. Through the hole, a faint glimpse of a sunny street. Dust drifts in the beam. No people. The mood is confined, patient, still.

## 9. `ch09_woods_meeting.png`

**Covers:** "On August 21st, he met his followers in the woods. That night, they began."

**Prompt:** Southampton County, Virginia, August 1831, night. Dense summer woods beside a small dark pond, with tall pines and thick undergrowth. The embers of a small cooking fire glow low on the ground. A few figures stand in deep shadow at the edge of the firelight, seen from behind, indistinct. No faces are visible. A sliver of moon behind clouds. The mood is tense and secretive.

## 10. `ch09_hiding_place.png`

**Covers:** "Turner hid in the woods for more than two months. On October 30th, a farmer found him."

**Prompt:** Southampton County, Virginia, late October 1831. Autumn woods with fallen leaves. A large fallen tree lies on the ground, its tangled branches covering a small dug-out hollow in the earth beneath it, just big enough for one person to hide in. The hollow is empty. Cool, gray morning light comes through bare branches. No people. The mood is quiet and hunted.

## 11. `ch10_house_of_delegates.png`

**Covers:** "In January 1832, Virginia's lawmakers spent two weeks debating it." It also covers the 73–58 vote.

**Prompt:** Richmond, Virginia, January 1832. The inside of a legislative chamber in the Virginia State Capitol: white-painted walls, tall windows with winter light, rows of wooden desks, and an elevated speaker's chair. Dozens of legislators in dark 1830s frock coats and high collars are seated, seen from behind and from the side, faces turned away or in shadow. One man stands at a desk in the middle distance, back to the viewer, mid-speech, one hand raised. Papers lie on the desks. The mood is serious and divided.

**Style paragraph** (paste at the end of every prompt, word for word):

> Style: an American oil painting from the 1830s–1850s in the manner of the period's genre painters, such as George Caleb Bingham and William Sidney Mount. Muted earth palette (umber, ochre, slate blue, dull red), warm directional light, visible brushwork, faint craquelure, slightly aged varnish. Every detail must be historically accurate for the stated year and place: clothing, hairstyles, buildings, furniture, lamps and tools. No text of any kind: no letters, words, signs, labels, captions, signatures, dates or watermarks. No frame or border. No recognizable real historical person. Faces, where visible, belong to ordinary anonymous people.
